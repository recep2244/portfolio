<p class="text-lg text-slate-400 leading-relaxed">
  <strong class="text-text">IntFOLD</strong> is an integrated server for the automated prediction of
  protein tertiary structure, functional sites, intrinsic disorder, and per-model quality — one
  pipeline that has served the research community for over a decade.
</p>
